Builds foundational generative AI skills using Microsoft Copilot for everyday business workflows. Progresses from Copilot Chat and effective prompting to content creation, data analysis, meetings, and collaboration, then covers responsible AI, Pages and Notebooks, agents, and Copilot Cowork through no-code scenarios.
